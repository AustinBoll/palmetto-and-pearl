Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

What is included with my reservation?

The main house includes 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms and 4 beds, with private use of the courtyard, putting green, fire pit and game room. Add the 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom guest house with 1 additional bed, living room, and full kitchen for an extra nightly fee.

Can I book the guest house separately?

No. Reservations are for the main house and private property amenities. The guest house is an optional upgrade for an additional nightly fee.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, with the guest-house upgrade. Dogs are permitted in the guest house, courtyard and game room, but not inside the main house. Please contact us before booking to confirm arrangements.

What family amenities are available?

Palmetto & Pearl will provide two cribs and a high chair, along with bikes, games and outdoor amenities designed to make family stays easier and more enjoyable.

What neighborhood amenities can guests use?

Information regarding available neighborhood amenities and their use will be provided onsite during your stay.

How do I get help using something at the property?

Our digital Guest Guide will include quick instructions and short videos. QR codes placed near selected appliances and amenities will take you directly to the information you need.
