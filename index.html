Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Can Palmetto and Pearl be rented separately?

Yes. Palmetto and Pearl can be reserved independently, subject to availability.

What is shared when the residences are booked separately?

The courtyard, putting green, fire pit and garage game room are shared between guests staying in Palmetto and Pearl when the residences are reserved separately.

Can we reserve the entire property?

Yes. Reserving Palmetto & Pearl together provides the 3-bedroom/3-bath main residence and 1-bedroom/1-bath guest residence, sleeping up to 12 adults, with exclusive use of the shared property amenities during that reservation.

What family amenities are available?

Palmetto & Pearl will provide two cribs and a high chair, along with bikes, games and outdoor amenities designed to make family stays easier and more enjoyable.

What neighborhood amenities can guests use?

Information regarding available neighborhood amenities and their use will be provided onsite during your stay.
